Macaron Box

Hot steaming macaron box. Eeay and fast food to make at home!

Ingredients:

  • 400g of Macaron
  • 400g of Minced beef
  • 2 Eggs
  • 5dl Milk

Steps:

  1. Brown the beef
  2. Boil the macarons
  3. Mix the egg with milk
  4. Put all the Ingredients together in a large casserole dish and mix well
  5. Cook in a 225 celcius oven for a 40 minutes or until golden brown
