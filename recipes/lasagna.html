Lasagna

Delicious lasagna

Classic Italian lasagna, just like your grandma used to make!

Ingredients:

  • 1kg Lasagna sheets
  • 1kg Minced meat
  • 1kg Tomato sauce
  • 400g White sauce
  • 200g Parmesan
  • 100g Chives

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta sheets
  2. Brown the minced meat
  3. Add tomato sauce to minced meat and let simmer for 30 minutes
  4. Add a layer of meat sauce to baking dish
  5. Add a layer of pasta sheets on top of the sauce
  6. Add white sauce and some parmesan cheese
  7. Repeat this for at least five more times
  8. Bake in the oven at 225 celcius for 50 minutes
  9. Add parmesan cheese and let rest for a 15 minutes
  10. Garnish with chives and serve!
