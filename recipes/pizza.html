Pizza

Perfect pizza for the date night!

This pizza will make you yearn for more!

Ingredients:

  • Dough
  • Water
  • Yeast
  • Salt
  • Tomato sauce
  • Pepperoni
  • Pineapple

Steps:

  1. Mix the yeast wiht dough and add water, knead for a 10 minutes
  2. Add salt after kneading
  3. Let the dough rise for at least eight hours in room temperature or for a 24 hours in fridge
  4. Shape the dough to balls
  5. Shape the balls to rounds with hand or pinroll
  6. Add:
    1. tomato sauce
    2. cheese
    3. toppings
  7. Bake for a 8 minutes at 250 celsius oven
